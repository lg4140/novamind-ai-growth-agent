# V4 User Discovery Plan (Not Yet Conducted)

## Hypotheses
Growth managers may prefer explicit plan confirmation before content generation; claim provenance and an exact-version approval could improve trust; excessive mandatory confirmation might instead reduce speed.

## Interview guide
Ask 5–8 practitioners to walk through a recent B2B campaign from idea to send. Record tools, handoffs, what they delegate to AI, misstatements that required correction, approvals, time budgets, trust and risk boundaries. Avoid directing their answers. Ask for actual artifacts or anonymized process examples when consented.

## Evidence rules
No fabricated interviews, quotes, willingness-to-pay or market size. Code behavior is not user desirability. Research results are a required future evidence source before claiming reduced labor or better trust.
