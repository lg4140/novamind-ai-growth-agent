# V4 Product Value Study Design — No Results Yet

**Research question:** On equivalent campaign briefs, does plan-first AI assistance reduce active operator effort while preserving claim accuracy and review quality relative to manual/V1/V3? 

**Design:** fixed scenario set of 8–12 briefs across KPI/audience/constraints; randomized counterbalanced task order; recruit practitioners; record work start/end, focused active edit intervals, revision count, approved final version, reviewer blind quality ratings, model and tool cost. Include a no-evidence scenario to test appropriate non-claims.

**Primary outcome:** active human minutes to approved claim-safe content; secondary: elapsed TAC, quality score, corrections/iteration, USD per approved version. Guardrails: 0 unauthorized sends and no factual claim without adequate support.

**Analysis:** Report descriptive per-task distributions and paired within-user contrasts. Use inferential intervals only after actual data collection and preregistration, checking for learning/order effects. Do not reuse synthetic email engagement as evidence of demand or product uplift.

**State:** protocol only, no participants or measured effect sizes.
